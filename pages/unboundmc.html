UnboundMC

UnboundMC is a one-click installer that sets up the No Chat Restrictions mod on a stock Minecraft install. Since 1.16.4, Minecraft can lock flagged and child accounts out of chat and the multiplayer menu — UnboundMC restores that access for non-technical players. Just double-click, pick your Minecraft version, and play: no command line, no Java setup, working with the official launcher and vanilla client on Windows, macOS, and Linux. It's client-side only, safe to re-run, and fully reversible. Built with Avalonia and .NET, and developed end-to-end with AI.

UnboundMC is an installer, not the mod, and is not affiliated with Aizistral. All credit for No Chat Restrictions goes to Aizistral.
