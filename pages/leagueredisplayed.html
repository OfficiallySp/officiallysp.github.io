League Redisplayed

Desktop app for browsing League of Legends splash art and setting it as your wallpaper. Browse by champion, skin line, or universe, pick a crop, apply in one click, and rotate on a schedule if you want it to change on its own. Open reimplementation of Riot's discontinued League Displays. Offline after the first run, with 2,100+ splash artworks across Windows, macOS, and Linux.
