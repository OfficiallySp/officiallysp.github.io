Legend of Peks

A free Adventure RPG made with RPG Maker MZ (default RTP) in under 100 hours for a subathon challenge for streamer IHasPeks. Explore a world of witty dialogue, crazy events, and community inside jokes, then fight foes in an Active Time Battle system — from snakes and crabs to pixies and monkeys.
